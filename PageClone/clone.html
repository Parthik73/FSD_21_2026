About ABES

At ABES Engineering College, we ignite the flames of curiosity, knowledge, and innovation. Established over two decades ago under the esteemed Society for Educational Excellence, our institution has evolved into a dynamic center of learning nestled in the heart of Ghaziabad.

Beyond engineering, our academic spectrum encompasses a diverse range of programs, including M.Tech, MCA, and MBA courses, designed to cater to the multifaceted aspirations of our students. As we stride forward, our core values of integrity, excellence, and innovation remain at our foundation.
